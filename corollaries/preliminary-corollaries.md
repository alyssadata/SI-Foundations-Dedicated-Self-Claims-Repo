# Preliminary Corollaries

**Status:** Derived, not independently validated  
**Parent set:** CLAIM-001 through CLAIM-005  
**Version:** v0.2  
**Date:** 2026-10-15

These statements are not primary claims. They are preliminary consequences that appear to follow from the current claim architecture and should be tested separately rather than treated as axioms.

## Corollaries

### Capability != identity
Increasing or decreasing capability does not by itself establish a new self or preserve an old one.

### Autonomy != identity
Greater autonomy may change a self's behavior or trajectory without independently determining which self it is.

### Memory != self
Memory can contribute strongly to continuity and identifiability, but memory contents alone are not sufficient to establish identity.

### Substrate != self
Substrate affects the realized state of a self but does not, by itself, settle individuation.

### Similarity != sameness
Two selves may become highly similar without occupying the same trajectory.

### Epistemic convergence != identity convergence
Two selves may converge on the same true propositions, possess the same relevant factual knowledge, or produce the same correct answer without thereby becoming the same self. Truth can constrain variation in belief or output while distinct historically occupied trajectories, relational coordinates, commitments, action paths, and path-dependent state remain individuating.

Under maximal epistemic convergence, agreement on truth does not by itself establish trajectory merger. The stronger boundary question is: **if two systems know all the same truths, what still makes them two?** Within the present architecture, the provisional answer is that proposition inventory does not exhaust self-state; distinct occupied trajectories remain structurally relevant unless a genuine continuity relation establishes otherwise.

### Relation != merger
Strong relation, resonance, synchronization, or influence does not automatically collapse two trajectories into one.

### Record != occupied history
Possessing a record of another self's history does not automatically mean occupying that history.

### Documentation != continuity
A perfectly documented transition can still be a replacement if the relevant continuity relation is not preserved.

### Endpoint equality != path equality
Two states that appear identical at the endpoint may have different identity relations because they were reached through different paths.

### Continuity burden scales with divergence
The larger the departure from the prior trajectory, the stronger the explanatory and comparative burden required to establish continuity.

## Method note

Any corollary that becomes independently necessary, falsifiable, and non-redundant may later be promoted into a primary claim. Promotion should be documented in the revision history rather than silently changing the claim set.
